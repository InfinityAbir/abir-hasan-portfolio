Account deletion

Delete your Blood Network BD account

  • Effective date: October 30, 2026
  • Android app
  • Developer: Abir Hasan

This page explains how to permanently delete your Blood Network BD account and the personal data attached to it. Deleting is permanent and cannot be undone.

  • Done in the app in under a minute
  • Or request it by email
  • Your data is removed, not just hidden
On this page
  1. Delete from the app
  2. Delete without the app
  3. What is deleted
  4. What may be kept
  5. Only want a break?
  6. Contact

1Delete from the app

  1. Sign in and open Edit Profile.
  2. Scroll to Privacy & data and tap Delete my account.
  3. Enter your password to confirm. Deletion happens immediately.

2Delete without the app

If you can no longer open the app, email us from the address on your account (or include the phone number you registered with) so we can verify the request.

Email a deletion request

We will confirm by reply and complete the deletion within 30 days.

3What is deleted

  • Your account details: name, phone number, email, password hash and profile picture.
  • Your donor profile: blood group, date of birth, gender, location, donation history and availability.
  • Your blood requests, including patient details, and every match attached to them.
  • Your notifications, push-notification token, eligibility answers and sign-in sessions.

4What may be kept

Your account record stays only as an anonymous placeholder with no name, phone number, email or picture, and no way to sign in. Reports made against an account may be kept to protect other users. Server logs are kept only for a short period.

5Only want a break?

You do not have to delete your account to stop being contacted. In the app, open My Donor Profile and set your availability to Unavailable.
