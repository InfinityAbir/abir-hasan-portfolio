Privacy Policy

Blood Network BD

  • Effective date: October 30, 2026
  • Android app
  • Developer: Abir Hasan

Blood Network BD (“we”, “our”, “the app”) helps people in Bangladesh who need blood find compatible donors nearby. This policy explains what the app collects, why, who can see it, and the choices you have. You can delete your account and data at any time.

  • No ads or trackers

1What we collect

We collect only what the app needs to connect people who need blood with willing donors.

  • Account: your name, phone number and password (stored only as a one-way hash on our server). An email address is optional.
  • Profile picture (optional): a picture you choose from your gallery. The app crops it to a small square image before uploading it to our server.
  • Donor profile (donors only): blood group, gender, date of birth, district, upazila, area or address, last donation date and availability. This is health-related information that you provide yourself.
  • Blood requests: hospital, blood group, number of units, urgency, the date blood is needed, a contact phone number, and optionally the patient's name, relation and notes.
  • Eligibility check: your answers to the donation-eligibility questions.
  • AI assistant: the messages you send to the in-app assistant (see section 4).
  • Device and diagnostics: a push-notification token, crash reports and basic device information (through Firebase Crashlytics), and server logs such as IP address and request time, kept for security and abuse prevention.

The app does not use GPS and does not collect your precise location: you choose your district and upazila yourself. It contains no advertising and no ad trackers.

2How we use it

  • To create your account and sign you in.
  • To match blood requests with compatible, available donors and to send you notifications about matches and request updates.
  • To run the eligibility check and the AI assistant.
  • To keep the service safe: reviewing reports, preventing abuse and fraud, and fixing crashes.

3Who can see your information

  • Other people using the app. Donor search shows a donor's first name, blood group, district, upazila and area, availability, verification status and profile picture. When you are matched with someone, you see their name. Phone numbers are shared only after the donor accepts the match; until then neither side receives the other's number.
  • Administrators can see account, request and report information so they can verify donors and handle reports.
  • Service providers that process data for us: Render (hosting of our server and website), Neon (hosting of our database), Google Firebase (push notifications and crash reports) and Groq (writes the AI assistant's replies).

We do not sell your personal data.

4AI assistant

The assistant is available to signed-in members. When you send a message, the app sends it, together with up to the last 10 messages of the current conversation for context, over HTTPS to our server. Our server forwards the text to Groq, which generates the reply. We do not save your conversations, and the app keeps them only while the chat screen is open. We limit how many messages one account can send per minute to prevent abuse.

Please do not enter sensitive personal or medical details (such as ID numbers, financial details or your health history) into the assistant. It is an AI: it can make mistakes and it is not a doctor.

5Permissions

PermissionWhy the app needs it
InternetTo talk to our server.
Network stateTo tell whether you are online.
NotificationsTo alert you about matches and request updates. On Android 13 and later the app asks for this after you sign in.

The app never asks for location, camera, contacts or storage permission. Choosing a profile picture uses Android's built-in photo picker, which gives the app only the one picture you select.

6Security

Everything the app sends uses HTTPS. Your sign-in tokens and the health details cached on your phone (donor profile draft and eligibility answers) are stored encrypted on the device with the Android Keystore. Passwords are stored only as one-way hashes, and sign-in attempts and AI messages are rate limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

7Reports and safety

You can report a donor or a requester from the app. Reports are reviewed by administrators, who may contact the people involved or restrict an account. A limited record of a report may be kept to protect other users, even after an account is deleted.

8Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Edit Profile → Delete my account), or without the app on the account deletion page. Deletion removes your profile, donor details, blood requests and matches, notifications, push tokens, eligibility answers and profile picture, and anonymises your account record. Server logs are kept only for a short period.

9Your choices

  • Set your availability to Unavailable to stop appearing as an available donor.
  • Edit your phone, email, password and picture in the app, or remove your picture.
  • Turn notifications off in your phone's settings.
  • Ask for a copy or correction of your data by email.

10Medical disclaimer

The app, its eligibility check and its AI assistant give general information only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice. Always confirm eligibility and safety with a qualified medical professional or a blood bank before donating or receiving blood.

11Children

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

12Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address with a new effective date, and significant changes will be announced in the app.
