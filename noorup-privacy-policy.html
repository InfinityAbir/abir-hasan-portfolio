NoorUp: Islamic Lifestyle — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026

NoorUp ("we", "our") is an offline-first Islamic lifestyle app. This policy explains how we handle information.

1. Data we do NOT collect

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. There is no sign-in, no advertising SDK, no analytics, and no Firebase in the app.

2. Location (optional, on-device only)

If you enable GPS for accurate prayer times, your coordinates are stored only on your device (app preferences) and used only for local prayer-time calculation. They are never sent to us or any third party. The city name shown may use Android's on-device Geocoder.

3. Notifications and widgets

Prayer reminders and the home-screen widget work entirely through local Android system alarms and notifications. No data leaves your device.

4. Optional AI companion

The AI assistant works offline with built-in answers by default. It contacts a server only if you personally add your own Gemini API key — in that case your question text is sent to Google's Gemini API under Google's terms.

5. Children

The app contains no user accounts, chat with strangers, or purchases, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
